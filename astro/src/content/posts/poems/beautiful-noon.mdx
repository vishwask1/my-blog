<pre class="poem">
A beautiful noon —
to converse with you.
Sun beneath clouds,
twigs swaying,
day crawling,
evening afar.

If only
you were here.

You are,
in fact, here.
But, if only
I could.

A beautiful noon
If only.
</pre>
