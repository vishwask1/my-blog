<pre class="poem">
My space - cluttered with books
my Canvas - littered with colors.
You are a misfit in here
as the lone tree
in the concrete jungle.

They are cutting down the tree
and, painting the walls green.

I'm in no such haste
feel at home,
take your time 
learn me as you will.

After a while
if you're still here
over a cup of Coffee 
we shall watch
the paint dry.
</pre>
