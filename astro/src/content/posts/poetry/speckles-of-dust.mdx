<pre class="poem">
In the vast cosmos,
where the sun and the moon
have positioned themselves
Or in the stationary clock
hanging in the air
what the hand points to
means nothing.
When I find myself the time
to stare at its irrelevance,
a silence within reverberates
deafening the noise outside,
in search of meaning.

Tiny particles of dust - you and me
swept away
by the slightest of the wind
to places where we
eventually settle
offering a protest - too feeble,
to merely convince the self.

But, on some days
as the rays of light fall, on a placid soul
transcending the patch of void in the air
into a canvas of spectacle,
some speckles of dust glitter 
brighter than the stars.
To kindle the heart
with tiny pleasures of life.
rendering meaning to existence
in a momentary bliss.

"A whole minute of bliss!
Is that really so little for the whole of a man's life?"
</pre>
