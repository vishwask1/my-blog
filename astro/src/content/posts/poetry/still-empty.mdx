<pre class="poem">
He looks into the letter box
beside the gate.

It is empty.

After a while, he checks again
and then again.

Still empty.

He returns
to his chair
facing the gate.

watching,
people who had left earlier
returning 
just as the flock of birds above,
in the fading light.

He sits until 
the last person
across the street 
has left.
</pre>
