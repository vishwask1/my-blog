To write was a dream — unfulfilled, like most dreams. But I write anyway. So here you’ll find my failed attempts, scattered across different topics, trying to make sense of things I couldn’t otherwise.

Writing has been a struggle — to find the right words; to find the right amount of words. But I've willingly endured it. If I've used fewer words to convey what I intended, it is because I couldn't find more of them, and if I've used more, it is because I couldn't do it with fewer of them. So don't mistake it for artistry — it is merely my struggle to find the right balance; my tussle with words or the lack of it.

To pay the bills, I code. Or at least I used to; now I just prompt.

Either way, I write. When you write to a machine, you search for solutions. When you write to a human soul, you search for answers.

I'm based out of Bangalore; roots from Mangalore. I'm currently working for Nokia and taking life as it comes...

Read and be bored.
