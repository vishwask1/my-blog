To write was a dream — unfulfilled, like most dreams are. But I wrote anyway. So here you'll find my failed attempts, scattered across different topics, trying to make sense of things I couldn't otherwise.

To earn my pie, I code. Or at least I used to. Now I just prompt.

Either way, I write. And here's what I've found: when you write to a machine, you search for solutions. When you write to a human soul, you search for answers.

Read and be bored.
